### Changed
- Sidebar rows lead with one icon instead of a folder plus a mark: a repository's worktrees fold
  under a chevron and hang off a guide line, and a ready agent shows a quiet ring, so an agent at
  rest no longer looks like a plain shell.
- A PC's name in the sidebar opens its details: its state, address, herdr version and last error,
  with Reconnect, Update bridge, Connect or Disconnect, Rename and Remove. The sliders button and
  the form that opened inside the list are gone.
