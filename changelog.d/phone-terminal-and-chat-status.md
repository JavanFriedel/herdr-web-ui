### Changed
- On a touch screen the terminal uses its full width: the history scrollbar no longer reserves a
  strip on the right, and floats over the edge only while the view is scrolled back.
- While an agent works, the chat's last line, right over the input, says "Working…" (or
  "Thinking…") and for how long; the turn's work block names its steps instead of repeating it.
  A phone keeps the transcript's end closer to the input.
